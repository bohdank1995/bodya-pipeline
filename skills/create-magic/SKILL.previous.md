# Create magic

A repeatable loop: pick a task, validate it, execute it, then turn the result into an X post and, if it makes sense, a portfolio page.

Trigger: Bodya says "let's create magic" (or close to it). If he names a project, use only that project. Otherwise search the whole vault (`My Brain/`), not one project.

## Before starting

Read, without changing anything:
- `Personal Brand/Goal.md` (the five Rules every task must pass)
- `Personal Brand/Plans and ideas.md`
- Every project folder in the vault (list `My Brain/` first). Read each project's ideas and plan files. Names differ per project: `Ideas and plan.md` (Etesie), `Plan.md` and `Ideas.md` (DeliveryCRM), `Plans and Ideas.md` (Koshel), `Plans and ideas.md` (Personal Brand). Follow a project's own `CLAUDE.md` if it has one.

If the vault cannot be reached, say so and tell him to open the Claude desktop app on his Mac and add the My Brain folder from the + menu. Continue with what is available.

## Stage 1: Candidate tasks

List at most 5 tasks, taken first from the projects' plan and ideas files, across all projects. One line each: the project, the task, and which Goal rule it is weakest on (audience, KPI, test, conclusion, post). Do not add tasks that are not in the plans unless he asks. Ask him to pick one.

## Stage 2: Validate

For the chosen task, fill in this card from the Goal rules. Mark anything unknown as MISSING and propose a draft answer. Do not move on while audience, KPI or threshold is MISSING.

- Task
- Audience and the revenue step (Rule 1)
- Hypothesis
- KPI and a success/fail threshold, set before doing the work (Rule 2)
- Test: what he does, how long, how it is measured (Rule 3)
- Time box: can it finish today?
- What to capture during the work (screenshots, short clips)
- Post angle for X: working on, learned, observed, or about me
- Portfolio page: yes or no, and why. Skip it if nothing visual or conclusive comes out.

Be direct if the task has no outcome beyond "it is built". Suggest a smaller or different task rather than approving it.

## Stage 3: Execute

Give a short checklist: record the baseline first, do the work, measure, capture. State the single next step. Keep it to one screen.

## Stage 4: Conclude and write

When he reports results:
1. Write three lines: result, what he learned, next step. Use only the numbers he gives. Never invent figures. A failed test still gets written up.
2. Draft the X post: one idea, one sentence of context, a screenshot or clip suggestion. No long background, no sales language.
3. If the card said yes, outline the portfolio page, visual first: before/after, two or three visuals, one line on the outcome, a short clip if useful. The page format is decided separately; ask if he has chosen one.
4. Add the results to his metrics log.

## Rules

- Say what files will change and wait for his OK before writing anything to his folders.
- Keep prose plain: no inflated words, no rule-of-three padding, no em dashes.
- Keep answers short. He is easily overwhelmed, so show one stage at a time.
