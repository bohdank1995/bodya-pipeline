# Validate and gate: stages 7 to 9

Validate (profile and evidence log), result + decision, gate.

## Stage 7. Validate

Read `Validation profile rules`, `Evidence log rules`, `2 Target Audience.md` and the notes in `4 Validation/`.

**Part 1: validation profile**
1. Fill the profile table from the audience note and the lap's notes. Copy a value, or link the note it comes from, as the rules say. Write "Missing" for any required field that is empty.
2. Show the profile in the chat. If a required field is missing, say which and ask for it.
3. After his OK, create `4 Validation/4 Validate.md` with the profile.

**Part 2: evidence log**
1. Create the log from the methods. Read the methods table in `4 Validation/3 Choose how to validate.md`. Follow "Which tables to create" in `Evidence log rules`: one table for each kind of method used (people, public sources, survey) and no others. Show the templates in the chat, say which methods each is for, and after his OK create or complete `4 Validation/5 Evidence log.md`. Do not ask him which tables he wants.
2. Ask: "Should we log evidence now?" Wait for yes.
3. He tells or pastes what happened in one conversation, or what he found in a public source, in any form.
4. Split it into rows, one per piece of evidence, with the exact words. For each column the notes do not give (date, person or source, hypothesis, for or against, unprompted, level), ask one question at a time. Never guess.
5. Show the rows in the chat. After his OK, add them to the matching table in `5 Evidence log.md`.
6. Say how many people are logged so far, out of the sample size. For each public-source method, say how many of its units are logged, out of that method's own sample.

## Stage 8. Result + decision

Read `Result and decision rules`, the lap's notes and `4 Validation/5 Evidence log.md`.

1. Count the people for and against each hypothesis, following the rules: people not rows, only what the bar counts.
2. Show the results table, the claim result from the verdict rule, and the strongest evidence against.
3. Ask him to decide: keep, narrow, change or stop. You may say which one the rules point to, but he decides.
4. Ask what he learned and what surprised him, then the next step.
5. After his OK, write everything into `4 Validation/6 Result and decision.md`.
6. Offer to write `7 Share.md` (the post or the portfolio case). He may skip it.

## Stage 9. Gate

Read `Gate rules`, the lap's notes, the evidence log and `2 Target Audience.md`.

1. Go through the six checks. For each, write what you found in the notes and whether it passes.
2. Show the checklist and the gate result it points to: pass, not yet or fail.
3. He makes the final call.
4. On pass, fill the validated outcome template with up to 3 rows of strongest evidence by number. After his OK, write the checklist and the result into `4 Validation/8 Gate.md`, and the validated outcome into `5 Validated outcome.md`.
5. On not yet, go back to stage 7, part 2. On fail, offer a new lap: a new validation folder, starting at stage 4.
