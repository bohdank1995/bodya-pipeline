# Plan the test: stages 4 to 6

Choose what to validate, hypotheses + bar, choose how to validate. Each has its own note in the lap's folder, `4 Validation/`.

## Stage 4. Choose what to validate

Read `Choose what to validate rules`, `1 Vision.md`, `2 Target Audience.md` and `3 Desired Outcomes.md`.

1. He picks one outcome.
2. Turn it into the statement: "We validate that [audience] [feel / experience / do this] when [situation]". Take the audience from the audience note exactly as written. Ask for the situation if the notes do not give it.
3. Fix anything that breaks the rules: two feelings in one statement, a wish instead of the present, a solution word, a number. Explain each fix in one line.
4. Show the statement and the checklist result. After his OK, create `4 Validation/1 Choose what to validate.md` with the outcome as a link to `3 Desired Outcomes` and the statement. Write no status. Leave the hypotheses and the methods for the next stages.

## Stage 5. Hypotheses + bar

Read `Hypothesis rules` and `4 Validation/1 Choose what to validate.md`.

1. Ask him to write his hypotheses plainly, in any form.
2. For each one, ask only the questions needed to fill the template: who exactly, what they do or feel, how often or how many, the number, the sample size, and the kind of evidence.
3. Put each in the template: "We believe that [audience] [does or experiences X] [how often, or how many]", with the success bar "We are right if [number] of [sample size] [show this]".
4. Check: 3 to 5 hypotheses, one behaviour each, about today, observable, different aspects (frequency, feeling, cost, workaround), a bar on every one. If an aspect is missing, say so and ask whether to add one.
5. If he gives no number, propose one and label it "proposed". Ask him to confirm or change it. A deadline goes in the validation profile, so leave it out here.
6. Ask for the rule for the whole claim, and propose one if he has none (for example, at least 3 of 4 reach their bar).
7. Show the table. After his OK, create `4 Validation/2 Hypotheses and bar.md` with the columns #, Aspect, We believe that, We are right if. Write no status column. Put the rule for the whole claim under the table.

When showing hypotheses for the first time, say that H means hypothesis and that later notes refer to them by number.

## Stage 6. Choose how to validate

Read `Choose how to validate rules` and `4 Validation/2 Hypotheses and bar.md`.

**Part 1: methods**
1. Show the list of methods from the rules, as a table, in the chat.
2. Suggest methods for the hypotheses, following the rules for choosing: match each method to its success bar, cheapest honest method, "do" over "say", at most two methods per lap.
3. Show the suggestion as the methods table from the rules: one table for all methods, grouped by method, the method named once, one row per hypothesis, columns in this order: Method, People, Tests, How, Bar, Sample, Needs. Start each How with a plain verb. Copy each bar from `2 Hypotheses and bar.md` without changing it.
4. He chooses. After his OK, create `4 Validation/3 Choose how to validate.md` with the methods table.

**Part 2: next steps (optional)**
1. Ask: "Should we now write the next steps?" Wait for yes. He may say no.
2. Draft 3 to 7 next steps as the next steps table from the rules: Step, Produces, Needs, Time box, Needs other people. Each step starts with a verb, holds one action and produces something visible. The first step can start today. Steps end where "Validate" starts. Mark time boxes as rough estimates.
3. Show the table in the chat. After his OK, write it into `3 Choose how to validate.md` under a "Next steps" heading.
