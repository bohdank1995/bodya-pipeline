# Share drafts

Write these after the Learning Card. A "not real" verdict is shared too: it's often the better story.

## X post

- **Hook:** the problem I suspected, in one line
- **What I did:** methods, in 1–2 lines
- **What I found:** the strongest evidence, with a quote or a number
- **Verdict + next step:**

Ideas for extra posts during the experiment:
- Before: "Here's a problem I think exists. Do you have it?" (doubles as a content test)
- During: one surprising quote or finding

## Portfolio case study

1. **Context:** project, milestone, why this problem matters for profit
2. **Hypothesis:** copy it from the hypothesis card
3. **Method:** what I did, and why these methods
4. **Evidence:** 3–5 strongest pieces, plus counter-evidence
5. **Verdict and decision**
6. **What I'd do differently**
