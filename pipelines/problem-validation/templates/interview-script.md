# Interview script (Mom Test style)

Ask about the past, not the future. Never pitch. Never ask "would you use…?"
Listen for specific stories, numbers and workarounds.

## Opening

- "I'm researching how people handle [area]. No product to sell. Can I ask about your last time dealing with it?"

## Core questions

1. "When was the last time you ran into [problem]? Walk me through what happened."
2. "How often does that happen?"
3. "What did it cost you: time, money or something else?"
4. "What have you tried to fix it?"
5. "What are you using right now? What do you dislike about it?"
6. "Have you paid for anything to deal with this? How much?"
7. "Who else do you know who deals with this?"

## Red flags (these are not evidence)

- Compliments ("great idea!")
- Hypothetical answers ("I would probably…")
- General statements ("I usually…", "I always…") with no specific story behind them

## After each interview

Log the quotes in the evidence log within 24 hours.
