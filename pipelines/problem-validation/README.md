# Pipeline: Problem validation

**Question:** Is the problem I'm assuming real, and is it painful enough to be worth solving?

Use this pipeline *before* building any solution. You build no product here, only evidence.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Output | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hypothesis | Who has the problem, what it is, how often, the current workaround | [hypothesis-card.md](templates/hypothesis-card.md) |
| 2 | Build | Choose 2–3 cheap methods and prepare them (search list, script, post) | [hypothesis-card.md](templates/hypothesis-card.md) (Plan section) |
| 3 | Measure | Every piece of evidence logged and scored against the 4 criteria | [evidence-log.md](templates/evidence-log.md) |
| 4 | Learn + Decide | Verdict: real, not real, or unclear, plus the next step | [learning-card.md](templates/learning-card.md) |
| 5 | Share | X post and portfolio case study drafts | [share.md](templates/share.md) |

## Methods (weakest → strongest evidence)

| Method | Real problem looks like |
|---|---|
| Survey | Many say "weekly or more often" with high pain. Use it to count a problem, not to discover it |
| Interviews ([Mom Test script](templates/interview-script.md)) | Specific, recent stories, told without being led |
| Community mining (Reddit, forums, X, 1–3★ reviews) | The same complaint repeated, unprompted, in people's own words |
| Search demand (Google Trends, keyword tools, autocomplete) | People actively search for a fix |
| Workaround hunting (spreadsheets, scripts, Upwork/Fiverr jobs) | People already spend time or money on it |
| Competitor check | Paying customers exist, and their reviews show gaps |
| Observation / diary | You watch the pain happen |
| Problem post (X / Reddit, no pitch) | "Same here" replies, saves, DMs |

Start with the cheapest methods that need no other people (community mining, search demand,
workaround hunting, competitor check). Talk to people once you know what to ask.

## Decision rule: the four "hair on fire" criteria

| Criterion | Evidence counts when… |
|---|---|
| **Frequent** | People hit the problem weekly or more often |
| **Painful** | It costs them time, money or stress that they describe without prompting |
| **Actively solved** | They have searched for, built or tried a fix |
| **Paid for** | They spend money or significant time on a workaround or a product |

- **3–4 criteria met** → the problem is real. Next: a solution-validation experiment.
- **2 criteria met** → unclear. Run one more lap with a stronger method or a narrower group of people.
- **0–1 criteria met** → not real for this group. Pivot to another group or another problem, or stop.

A criterion counts as met only when **at least 3 independent sources** back it up
(different people or posts, not the same person twice).
